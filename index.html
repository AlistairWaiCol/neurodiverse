What I offer

Support that fits real life.

🧩

Assessments

Clear, supportive assessments that help you understand how your child learns and what they need. They can also help you find your way through the health system if you are seeking a medical diagnosis.

🏡

Support plans for school and home

Practical, personalised strategies that work in real classrooms and real family life. Small adjustments that help your child feel capable and confident.

💡

ADHD workshops

In-person sessions for parents and for teachers on understanding and supporting ADHD. Free, and about an hour to an hour and a half each.

Hi, I'm Alistair

Educator, former principal, and neurodivergent myself.

Meet Alistair

This work is personal.

I'm an educator with experience as a primary school principal, and as a teacher in primary, secondary and university settings. I've spent about ten years in learning support and have psychology training.

I'm also dyslexic and have ADHD, and my partner is autistic. In my very first year of teaching, I noticed children who were struggling and struggling, and who just needed things done a little differently. It showed me how much small changes can matter.
