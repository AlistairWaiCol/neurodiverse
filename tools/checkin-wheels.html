EF Wheel — Student Check-in

A quick check-in about how things feel for you day to day. Internal record only — not a standardised or diagnostic result.

For each one below, move the slider to show how true it feels for you: 0 = not true for me at all, 10 = really true for me. Go with your first instinct rather than overthinking it — there's no right answer.

For the supporting adult: read each item aloud if that helps, and let the student lead — this is their view of themselves, not yours. Once submitted, download the record below and file it in line with your school's retention policy.
